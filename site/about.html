Meet the Team

Meneses Group is built on two complementary strengths: a decade of top-producing bi-coastal sales experience, and a legal, detail-driven approach to every transaction. Based in Los Angeles and Miami, we bring white-glove representation to every client we serve.

Co-Founder, Meneses Group

Brandon Meneses

Brandon is a bi-coastal luxury real estate expert with over a decade of experience and just under $200 million in career sales. He launched his career at just 17 years old as an assistant with The Alexander Group in Boca Raton, rising to Director of Operations and then one of the team's top-producing agents before establishing himself as a top agent in Miami. He relocated to Los Angeles in 2019, where he continues to thrive in the city's competitive luxury market.

Brandon has been nationally recognized in the Real Trends Verified Top 1% of Realtors year after year, first achieving the distinction at just 24 years old — alongside honors including Rookie of the Year at 19 and Los Angeles Magazine Real Estate All Star. His standout sales include Adam Lambert's Hollywood Hills residence, Villa Amodei (the highest sale in Somis history), and 1237 Bel Air Rd, sold with multiple offers for $6.7 million. He has closed sales across Beverly Hills, Bel Air, Pacific Palisades, West Hollywood, Malibu, and beyond.

Co-Founder, Meneses Group

Janelle Meneses

Janelle brings a unique blend of legal acumen, linguistic versatility, and a deep passion for real estate to Meneses Group, serving the luxury market from Palm Beach to Miami. Before real estate, she worked at a Boca Raton real estate law firm handling multi-million dollar transactions, and currently interns in commercial transactions at Hinshaw Culbertson in Miami — experience that lets her thoroughly walk clients through every step of a deal and provide comprehensive protection throughout.

She specializes in luxury real estate, including new developments and condominiums. During law school, she served as President of the Association of Business Law Students and founded the school's Blockchain and Cryptocurrency Organization. Outside of work, Janelle can be found practicing yoga, meditation, and pilates, exploring new cuisines, or volunteering with animal rescue at Tri-County Animal Rescue.
